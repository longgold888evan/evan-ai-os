# Career Strategy

Primary target roles: AI/ML engineering roles with strong product and systems exposure, especially MLE and FDE-style positions.

## Core capability stack
- Machine learning engineering
- LLM/agent systems
- Agent orchestration
- System design
- Production AI systems
- Evaluation and observability
- Communication and solution design

## Principle
Prefer a small number of high-value learning resources and projects over broad course accumulation. Convert learning into artifacts, implementations, and interview-ready stories.
